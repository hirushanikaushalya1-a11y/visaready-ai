Frequently asked questions

Does VisaReady AI guarantee my visa?

No. Nobody can. Decisions are made by immigration authorities. We help you prepare a complete, consistent application and understand potential weaknesses.

What does the readiness score mean?

It measures how complete and consistent your application preparation is — profile answers, required documents, evidence for your claims and consistency between documents. It is not a probability of approval.

Where do the requirements come from?

Only from official government and immigration-authority sources. Each requirement shows its source link and the date our team last verified it. Where we have not verified something, we say so.

Is my data safe?

Documents are encrypted with a unique key per file, never publicly accessible, and deleted 30 days after you complete your application (or sooner on request). We never sell your data or use it to train AI without your explicit consent.

Can AI check if my documents are genuine?

No. We check readability and consistency between documents. We cannot verify authenticity with issuing authorities, and we say so on every document.

Is this legal advice?

No. VisaReady AI is not a law firm or a licensed immigration adviser. Where your situation needs professional judgement (for example previous overstays) we tell you.
